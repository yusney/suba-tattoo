## Colour with character

Illustrative Color combines bold outlines and saturated colour with influences from comics, folklore and underground illustration. At SUBA TATTOO, a tattoo studio in Montcada i Reixac (Barcelona), the linework organises every area before the colour goes in, and the colour is built up in layers for a solid, expressive finish rather than a washed-out gradient.

## From folklore to comics

The style covers very different registers. 'Daruma and Clouds' reinterprets the traditional Japanese charm with clouds and green leaves framing the figure. 'Maneki Neko Golden Mane' turns the lucky cat into a guardian with a lion's mane, finished with flat colour and defined outlines. At the other end of the spectrum, 'Cartoon Zombie' goes for the humour and energy of an original illustrated character.

What these pieces share is composition: every element has its place, colour separates the planes and the orientation of the design follows the anatomy, whether on the thigh, calf, forearm or back.

## Made to last

To help the colour keep its intensity, we build saturation through layers of pigment and rely on black to separate planes instead of diluting it. After the session, aftercare makes the difference: washing with neutral soap, applying balm in thin layers and protecting the tattoo from the sun, especially during the first weeks.
