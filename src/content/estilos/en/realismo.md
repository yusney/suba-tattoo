## Realism with photographic volume

At SUBA TATTOO, a tattoo studio in Montcada i Reixac, just outside Barcelona, we see realism as a matter of light and volume. Portraits, animals, anatomy and nature are built in greyscale or colour through soft gradients, without hard outlines that would break the sense of a real image. The deepest black is kept for the points of highest contrast, so the piece never looks muddy and stays readable as time goes by.

## Designed around your anatomy

A realism tattoo is not drawn on flat skin: it adapts to muscle, bone and movement. That is why every design is made to measure, taking into account the area of the body and the size needed for the detail to breathe. One example is "Wolf in Night Forest", a 30x40 cm black and grey scene running across the shoulder and forearm: the wolf, the pine forest and the moon were planned so the composition stays coherent when the arm moves.

You are welcome to bring references, such as photos or images that inspire you. The artist reinterprets them with technical durability criteria, so the result works on skin and not just on a screen.

## Aftercare and ageing

The surface of the skin regenerates in 2 to 3 weeks, and the deeper dermis stabilises after roughly 45 to 60 days. During that time, following the post-session aftercare and keeping the tattoo out of the sun are what most influence how well the tones of a realism piece hold up.
