## Dark iconography and symbolism

Dark art explores the most unsettling side of imagery: disturbing characters, esoteric symbols and scenes with a heavy atmosphere. At SUBA TATTOO, a tattoo studio in Montcada i Reixac (Barcelona), we work it mostly in black and bare skin, with deliberate chiaroscuro that concentrates tension on the key points of the composition.

## Expression over accumulation

A good dark art piece does not need to fill every centimetre with detail. In 'Sinister Clown', the work focuses on the face and the twisted smile, with the sunken eyes as the point of maximum tension. In 'Scarecrow', the figure emerges from a mist built with open textures: the face and hands keep their detail, while the surroundings dissolve into soft gradients.

Other pieces lean on symbolism, such as 'Baphomet', where the central figure is combined with a pentagram and sacred geometry in an ordered structure that reveals itself layer by layer.

## Contrast that lasts

Large masses of black and well-reserved highlights of bare skin are the foundation for these pieces to keep their strength over time. The composition adapts to the anatomy, usually vertically on the thigh, calf or forearm, so the figure follows the muscle without distorting as the body moves. After the session, following the aftercare and protecting the tattoo from the sun are essential.
