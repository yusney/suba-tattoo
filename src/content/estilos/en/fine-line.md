## Precision in every line

Fine line is built on thin lines, clean strokes and airy compositions. At SUBA TATTOO, a tattoo studio in Montcada i Reixac, near Barcelona, we work it with very fine liner needles and continuous black, without fills or shading competing with the drawing. The aim is for every piece to have the elegance of an editorial illustration and to stay readable as time goes by.

## Delicate designs with character

The simplicity of fine line demands a great deal of precision. In 'Harry Potter Glasses', on the inner forearm, two frames, the bridge and the lightning bolt are enough to create a detail that fans recognise instantly and everyone else finds elegant. In 'Butterflies, Heart and Stars', the elements are spread around the ankle like a small constellation, linked by their scale and by a single line weight.

Each composition adapts to its placement: the larger elements sit where there is more surface and the small details fill the spaces in between.

## Caring for a fine line tattoo

The surface of the skin regenerates in 2 to 3 weeks and full stabilisation takes roughly 45 to 60 days. With fine line, following the post-session instructions is especially important: washing with neutral soap, applying a very thin layer of balm and avoiding direct sun and swimming in pools or the sea during the first days.
