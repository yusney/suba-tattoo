## Symmetry and millimetric precision

Geometric tattooing applies the logic of mathematics to the skin: symmetry, patterns, edges and defined planes. At SUBA TATTOO, a tattoo studio in Montcada i Reixac, very close to Barcelona, we work it with precise outlines that organise every area before filling, so the structure of the design reads clearly from any distance.

## Reinterpreted motifs

Geometry is not just for mandalas or abstract patterns. It can also translate a recognisable motif into a different visual language. In 'Bowser', on the outer calf, the character's silhouette is broken down into angular planes and areas of colour that break up the roundness of the original design. In 'Koala and Flowers', on the thigh, triangles and clean lines fragment the volume without losing the animal's gentle expression.

In both cases, the geometry does not erase the likeness: it transforms it into a contemporary piece, balancing figure and structure.

## Anatomy matters too

Straight lines on a curved surface require planning. Each design is placed with the muscle and movement in mind, so the symmetry does not distort when the body moves. After the session, the usual aftercare, washing with neutral soap, applying balm in thin layers and protecting the piece from the sun, helps keep the lines clean.
